Q: How can I get an update when a new post comes out?

A: Subscribe to the RSS feed.

Q: What are these posts?

A: Learning notes. Each post digests a topic (often a course chapter or a set of papers) into one self-contained write-up, with derivations filled in and references at the end.

Q: What if I see something incorrect in a post?

A: Please let me know! Corrections are always welcome, and fixed posts get a one-line [Updated on YYYY-MM-DD: ...] note in blue at the top.

Q: How is this site built?

A: A small Python script turns HTML fragments into static pages. The layout follows the Hugo PaperMod theme, math is rendered with MathJax and the figures are inline SVG.
