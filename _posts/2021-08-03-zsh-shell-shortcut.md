## Useful keys

- forward (completion)/ backward: `Ctrl-F`, `Ctrl-B`
- Up/ Down history: `Ctrl-P`, `Ctrl-N`
- Delete: `Ctrl-?`
- Delete line/ word: `Ctrl-U`, `Ctrl-W`
- Search in reverse order: `Ctrl-R`
- Other bindings: `bindkey`
- zsh builtins manual: `man zshzle`
